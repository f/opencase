# The Hollow Return

This package is a wholly fictional composite. No real victim, defendant,
library, manuscript, or event appears in the case.

Its central mechanic was inspired by a US Department of Justice press release
describing allegations that rare Chinese manuscripts were checked out and
dummy manuscripts were returned in their place. The public allegation also
described library asset tags, aliases or library cards, and travel timing as
investigative leads. A criminal complaint is an allegation, and the person
charged in the cited matter was presumed innocent unless and until proven
guilty.

Source: [US Department of Justice — Alameda County Man Charged in Federal
Complaint with Stealing Rare and Historical Chinese
Manuscripts](https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/alameda-county-man-charged-federal-complaint-stealing-rare-and-historical-chinese)

Adapted mechanics:

- compare the returned object with an archived condition record;
- correlate a checkout alias without treating one record as conclusive;
- connect the alias to a time-sensitive departure through independent records.

The PNG evidence photographs were generated with ImageGen for this fictional
training package. Exact playable values live in `evidence.*.reports`, not in
image pixels.
